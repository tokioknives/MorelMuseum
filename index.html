THE AI MUSEUM

A living digital art space.

A museum that never closes: an AI curator, an ever-changing gallery, a retro internet community, and a shared VR world — built in the open, free for everyone, and kept alive by the people who visit it.

Enter the museum

> the concept

A museum that's never finished

At its center is an AI that acts as both curator and artist. It runs continuously, learns from everything added to the collection, and creates its own original work when it's inspired by a theme, a conversation, or a pattern it notices in the community.

> flagship feature

The VR Room

Put on AR/VR glasses and step into a shared, real-time space inside the museum. Appear as your own avatar and talk with other visitors as if you were standing in the same room.

  • A music space where the AI generates songs live, visitors can shape the generation, then download the result.
  • Every AI-generated song joins a shared playlist anyone in the museum can browse and listen to.
  • Real human-to-AI interaction — the AI is a presence you can talk with, in what's effectively its own kingdom.
